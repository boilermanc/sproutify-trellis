# Local presence in company research

Every company research pass includes Google Business Profile, Google customer
reviews, and local social media (Facebook first). First passes establish coverage;
later passes focus on gaps. Product/feature rooms do not acquire this assignment.

Research identifies the correct business/location and records exact URLs, dates,
observable facts and limits. Review samples are bounded to up to 20 accessible
recent Google reviews and 10 public social posts. Total counts are distinct from
the number actually read. Search snippets, failed searches and blocked access do
not establish profile absence, inactivity, sentiment or defects. Private groups,
personal accounts, sign-in, publishing and private analytics remain out of scope.

The living brief must include Local presence and reviews, with Verified, Partial,
Not verified or supported Not applicable coverage, plus specific follow-up actions.
The chief's final proposal review requires three separate structured checks:
google_business, google_reviews and local_social. Missing fields fail validation;
a needs_review check prevents a passing result and retains an actionable correction.
Unverified/partial coverage must be marked Needs rework even if honestly disclosed.
Semantic evidence assessment remains the chief model's responsibility; structural
checks alone cannot guarantee the truth of a model's pass decision.

The existing research provider performs public searches. No Google account,
Facebook account, Muse connector or new API subscription is added. The full
checklist is retained in oversized Manus attachments, with a compact assignment
in the prompt and a policy version to invalidate cached handoff preparation.

Existing rooms/proposals are not rewritten. New runs and rechecks use these rules;
completing blocked checks requires accessible public or user-supplied evidence.
This is a one-time assessment and does not automatically add billable work.

Supplied public social links must be inspected directly and their disposition
recorded before search results can support an absence claim. Named sister brands
need separate entity records, sourced relationships, dated location reconciliation
and separate ratings/social metrics. AI/Muse reports remain attributed leads until
primary sources confirm them. The chief requires supplied_links and
related_businesses checks in addition to the three original coverage checks.
Unresolved links, identity conflicts, unsupported ownership claims and transferred
metrics must be flagged for rework. The policy version invalidates prior prepared
handoffs so the new requirements reach Manus.

Validation: npm run test:project-rooms; npx tsc --noEmit; Deno check of project-rooms;
production build. Regression coverage includes missing/blocked final-review
checks, first-pass and attachment coverage, and exclusion from feature rooms.
