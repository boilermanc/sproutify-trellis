# Rekkrd Founder Letter

This is the reusable companion to `rekkrd-founder-letter.html`. The database migration installs it only for the `rekkrd` branch. In Campaign Builder, selecting **Rekkrd Founder Letter** turns each `{{token}}` below into an editable field; `{{first_name}}` and `{{unsubscribe_url}}` are filled automatically per recipient.

## First campaign

**Subject:** Why I built Rekkrd (and we're now on iOS)

**Preview text:** Rekkrd is now native on iPhone — and I wanted to share why I built it.

| Field | Initial value |
| --- | --- |
| Intro Copy | I wanted to send you a quick note as one of the first people using Rekkrd — thank you for that. It means a lot to have real collectors testing something I built out of a pretty specific itch. |
| Story Copy | I collect vinyl, and I collect gear — turntables, amps, cartridges, all of it. Discogs is great for tracking what I own, but it was never built to help me think about my listening life — what I actually spin, how my gear chain is set up, what a record is worth to sell versus keep. So I started building Rekkrd as the layer on top of Discogs that does the things Discogs was never trying to do. |
| Positioning Copy | To be clear: Rekkrd isn't trying to replace Discogs. Keep Discogs as your system of record — Rekkrd adds the listening journal, the gear catalog (we call it Stakkd), and the value tracking that pulls from both Discogs and eBay data. |
| Announcement Label | One more thing |
| Announcement Headline | Rekkrd just launched on the iOS App Store. |
| Announcement Copy | Everything you've been using in the browser now works natively on your phone — your collection, Stakkd, the listening journal, all of it, easier to reach for when you're at the record store or standing in front of your shelf. |
| CTA Text | Download on the App Store |
| CTA URL | Paste the final Rekkrd App Store product URL here before testing. |
| Supporting Copy | If you download it, a rating or quick review would genuinely help right now — at this stage, a handful of reviews from people who actually use the app makes a real difference for how it gets discovered. |
| Feedback Copy | You're getting to shape this thing early. If something feels off, missing, or half-baked — tell me. I read every reply. |

## Send checklist

1. Apply the migration, then open **Brand Intelligence → Email Templates → Rekkrd** and confirm the template preview.
2. In Campaign Builder, scope the campaign to the Rekkrd branch and select **Rekkrd Founder Letter**.
3. Paste the values above, using the real App Store product URL.
4. Send a test to yourself and check desktop, mobile, CTA, first-name fallback, and unsubscribe behavior.
5. Launch only after the Rekkrd consent audience and suppression filters are confirmed.

For later sends, keep the template and replace only the subject, preview text, editable copy, and CTA fields.
