# Rejoice Topic Clusters

This is a human-readable map, not the canonical topic registry. Add concrete questions to `topics.jsonl` and reference their IDs here after research.

## Current strategic landscape

- **How you feel:** precise emotional moments and questions without reducing the audience to crisis.
- **Curious about scripture:** context, questions, discovery, and approachable study.
- **Everyday wisdom:** relationships, work, decisions, patience, and ordinary responsibilities.
- **Growing in faith:** sustainable habits, reflection, and beginning again without a scoreboard.
- **Joy and gratitude:** celebration, friendship, music, and ordinary delight.
- **Rest and presence:** breathing room and reflection without medical or transformation claims.
- **Hard days:** dignified support and companionship without melodrama.
