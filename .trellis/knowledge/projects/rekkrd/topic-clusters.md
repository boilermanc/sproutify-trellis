## Current strategic landscape

- **Collector moments:** in the shop, at a fair, unpacking, moving, merging, or inheriting a collection.
- **Knowing the shelf:** duplicates, drift, forgotten records, lending, and collection visibility.
- **Collection workflow:** respectful interoperability, two-way Discogs sync, and one collection across tools.
- **Listening ritual:** choosing, handling, playing, sharing, and rediscovering records.
- **Collection identity and value context:** understanding what someone owns without unsupported valuation claims.
- **Rekkrd After Dark:** long-form mood music, listening-room programming, and series discovery; keep distinct IDs from core product content.
