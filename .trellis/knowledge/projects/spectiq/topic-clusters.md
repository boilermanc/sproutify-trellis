# SpectIQ Topic Clusters

This is the readable map for canonical topics in `topics.jsonl`.

## Inquiry to estimate

The buyer's live question, the inputs needed to price it, company-specific rules, and how an operator can explain the result.

## Inquiry to booking

The handoffs among estimate, agreement, payment, calendar, and the next action without pretending every exception can be automated.

## Auditable operations

Visible rules, histories, approvals, exceptions, and the practical value of knowing why a workflow made a decision.

## Office operations

Shared status and next-action clarity for owners, inspectors, and office staff.

## Buyer evaluation

Evidence-led criteria for comparing business software while avoiding hype, fabricated benchmarks, and competitor attacks.

## Coverage notes

The initial registry covers six positioning-backed questions. Add new topics only when product evidence, customer questions, search evidence, or reviewed founder observations support them.
