# Rekkrd Content Strategy

## Audience and goal

Rekkrd (`rekkrd.com`, `@rekkrdapp`) serves vinyl collectors who care about the ritual and want a clearer relationship with what they own. Content should earn trust with collectors, create qualified product interest, and support discovery without sounding like generic SaaS marketing.

## Positioning

Rekkrd is a collection-management companion with two-way Discogs sync, not a Discogs replacement. Respect the system collectors already use. Lead with real collection moments and let the product answer the friction.

## Voice

- Tactile, confident, unhurried: a good record shop, not a landing-page funnel.
- Short and declarative; no hype or exclamation marks.
- Speak to rituals and collection friction before features.
- Never disparage Discogs or fabricate statistics, valuations, URLs, handles, or claims.
- Avoid “revolutionize,” “effortless,” “seamless,” and repetitive feature-listing.

## Channel priorities

- Instagram: collector moments, tactile culture, collection friction, and product curiosity.
- Search/web: durable questions about collection workflows, identity, discovery, valuation context, and using complementary tools.
- LinkedIn/product stories: concrete workflows and build/product decisions only when relevant to a professional audience.
- YouTube/Rekkrd After Dark: music/listening content is a distinct series and should receive distinct topics/posts even though it shares the project partition.

## Content that matters

In-shop uncertainty, duplicate buys, moves and inherited collections, listening rituals, knowing what is on the shelf, collection identity, and respectful interoperability with established collector workflows.
