# Rejoice Content Learnings

This is the distilled, project-specific playbook. It starts intentionally empty: existing creative rules are strategy, not measured findings. Promote a learning only after reviewing task and canonical performance evidence.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Concise finding

- **Finding:** A bounded recommendation.
- **Evidence:** `task_id`, `experiment_id`, `post_id`, and relevant `event_id` values.
- **Confidence:** low | medium | high
- **Conditions:** Audience, channel, format, timing, and known limits.
- **Next use:** How a future Rejoice task should apply or retest it.
