# Rejoice SEO and Social Rules

## Topic selection

- Start with a real feeling, moment, objection, or question rather than a broad category.
- Map one primary question to one topic ID; related phrasing belongs in notes or the cluster summary.
- Prefer evidence from search queries, app/customer questions, comments, and recurring support themes.
- Cover the full emotional range: joy, curiosity, wisdom, growth, gratitude, rest, doubt, and hard days.

## Search assets

- Answer the primary query plainly before expanding into reflection or scripture context.
- Separate pastoral sensitivity from medical claims; do not frame the app as treatment.
- Cite/verify scripture and avoid fabricated wording.
- Define an honest next step that matches intent; do not force a signup into every answer.

## Social posts

- Name the precise moment. “The dread before a week you do not want” is stronger than “find peace.”
- One batch uses one explicit axis of variation: emotion, moment, objection, entry point, or grammatical form.
- Card headlines should generally fit one line; footers should stay near six words/40 characters.
- No visible fabricated scripture or invented app UI in generated imagery.
- Evaluate saves/shares and qualified profile action, not reach alone.

## Good hypotheses

A good Rejoice hypothesis connects specificity or framing to an observable behavior: for example, a concrete nightly question will earn more saves than generic encouragement among the same audience. Declare the comparison and time window.

Do not treat a Rekkrd result—or any other project result—as a Rejoice baseline.
