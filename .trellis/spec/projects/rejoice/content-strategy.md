# Rejoice Content Strategy

## Audience and goal

Rejoice (`letsrejoice.app`) serves people who want to explore scripture from the reality of how they feel: curious, grateful, restless, joyful, uncertain, or carrying a hard day. Content should make beginning feel human and accessible, then connect that lived moment to thoughtful study.

Primary goals are qualified awareness, trust, app exploration, and repeat engagement with useful scripture-centered content. Do not reduce the audience to people in crisis.

## Positioning

“Bible study for how you feel.” Rejoice meets the person at a specific emotion or everyday moment and moves toward scripture without guilt, pressure, or a spiritual scoreboard.

## Voice

- Warm, direct, dignified, and specific; never chirpy or solemn by default.
- Permission before instruction.
- No guilt, shame, guaranteed transformation, or claims about what scripture will do to someone.
- Avoid generic comfort language and tired growth clichés such as “journey,” “unlock,” “transform,” and “your best life.”
- Verse references must genuinely fit. Exact verse text must come from the licensed server-side source, never model memory.

## Channel priorities

- Instagram: specific emotional moments, useful questions, saves/shares, and app curiosity.
- Search/web: durable questions about scripture, emotions, reflection, and starting a study practice.
- Email: consent-aware follow-up and useful reflection for an existing relationship.

## Content that matters

Specific questions and situations; ordinary joy and curiosity; practical wisdom; sustainable study habits; rest and presence; support for difficult moments without melodrama. Content must remain broader than anxiety or sadness.
