# SpectIQ SEO and Social Rules

## Topic selection

- Begin with a real operator question, workflow bottleneck, or skeptical objection.
- Require product evidence, search evidence, customer language, or a reviewed founder observation.
- Keep SpectIQ separate from Farm leads, Farm sequences, and estimate-sales messaging.
- Do not imply a spoke integration; SpectIQ is a Trellis-native brand and its tenant data remains in SpectIQ.

## Search assets

- Answer the operational question before presenting SpectIQ.
- Map one primary query and one buyer intent to each durable asset.
- Use factual, scoped comparisons and only verified capabilities.
- Prefer diagrams of status, rules, and handoffs over vague “all-in-one” claims.
- Measure qualified product actions and early-access conversations, not traffic alone.

## Social posts

- One card, one operational idea, one low-pressure next step.
- Vary operator, workflow bottleneck, objection, proof type, or grammatical form—not all at once.
- Use short, declarative copy that can survive a skeptical owner's follow-up question.
- Lock visuals to orange/ink/paper, Public Sans, and the one-orange-room floor-plan mark.
- Prohibit gradients, retired lens imagery, fake dashboards, fabricated testimonials, and unsupported stats.

## Email

- General updates use the versioned SpectIQ template and branch-specific sender.
- Every send uses the Sweetwater Technology LLC mailing address, privacy link, and Hub-scoped unsubscribe handling.
- Production prospect outreach stays disabled until the dedicated Prospecting data model, workflow, and approvals are implemented.

## Good hypotheses

A good hypothesis predicts a qualified behavior from one operational framing—for example, an audit-trail explanation will produce more qualified demo requests than an “automation saves time” claim over the same window. Declare the audience, comparison, window, and approved metrics before publishing.
