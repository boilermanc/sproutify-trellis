# SpectIQ Content Learnings

Promote only reviewed findings supported by SpectIQ evidence. Farm results and assumptions are not transferable evidence.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Concise finding

- **Finding:** A bounded recommendation.
- **Evidence:** SpectIQ task, experiment, post, and performance event IDs.
- **Confidence:** low | medium | high
- **Conditions:** Audience, channel, format, timing, and limits.
- **Next use:** How a future task should apply or retest it.
