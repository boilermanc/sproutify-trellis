# SpectIQ Content Strategy

## Audience and goal

SpectIQ serves owners, operators, and office teams at home inspection companies. Content should earn a skeptical operator's trust, clarify the inquiry-to-booking problem, and create qualified early-access conversations without importing Farm audiences or tactics.

## Positioning

SpectIQ is home inspection business software built around one auditable path from inquiry through estimate, agreement, payment, and booking. It preserves company rules, visible exceptions, and human judgment. It is not an inspection-report writer, a consumer marketplace, or a promise to automate every business decision.

## Product description

SpectIQ connects estimates, agreements, payments, scheduling, and next actions so the company can see what happened, why it happened, and what needs attention next.

## Voice

- Professional and founder-led: an operator speaking plainly to another operator.
- Direct, evidence-led, and specific about workflows, rules, decisions, and exceptions.
- Respect skepticism; answer objections without belittling existing tools or processes.
- Distinguish current capabilities from planned work.
- Never claim guaranteed growth, conversion lift, revenue, time savings, or performance without approved evidence.
- Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changer,” “seamless,” “effortless,” “magic,” and generic AI hype.

## Visual system

- Ink `#1B1F27`, signal orange `#E56A2C`, and paper `#FAF8F5`.
- Public Sans for headings and body copy.
- Use the official floor-plan mark with exactly one orange room.
- No gradients, magnifying-glass marks, house-lens imagery, glossy SaaS abstractions, or unapproved logo variants.

## Approved hooks

- Win the inspection while the buyer is still on the phone.
- One clear path from inquiry to inspection.
- Automation with an audit trail and a way out.

## Channel priorities

- Search/web: durable answers to inspection-company workflow and software-evaluation questions.
- Founder-led LinkedIn and email: concrete operating problems, product decisions, and evidence-backed demonstrations.
- Visual social: one workflow problem or objection per card, using the locked brand system.
- Prospect outreach: disabled until the dedicated Prospecting workflow and approval gates exist.

## Content that matters

Live inquiry pricing, explainable company pricing rules, connected agreements and payments, correct booking, visible next actions, adoption risk, exception handling, and practical software evaluation.
